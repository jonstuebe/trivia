# Trivia

A single-player, Millionaire-style trivia game where the player picks a topic before playing.

## Topics

**Category**:
The top level of topic choice: General, TV Shows or Movies.
_Avoid_: Genre, section

**Title**:
One specific TV show or movie within a Category.
_Avoid_: Show, film, subcategory

**Topic**:
What the player picks to play: a whole Category, or one Title.
_Avoid_: Subject, theme

**Mixed game**:
A Game played on a whole TV Shows or Movies Category, drawing Questions from every Title in it.
_Avoid_: Category game, random game

## Play

**Game**:
One run up the Ladder, ending when the player wins, misses or walks away.
_Avoid_: Round, session, match

**Ladder**:
The 15 Rungs of a Game, each worth more money than the last.
_Avoid_: Money tree, levels

**Rung**:
One step of the Ladder: a position (1–15) and its prize.
_Avoid_: Level, stage

**Safe level**:
A Rung (5 and 10) whose prize is kept even after a later wrong answer.
_Avoid_: Checkpoint, milestone, safe haven

**Walk away**:
Ending a Game by choice to keep the current winnings.
_Avoid_: Quit, cash out, forfeit

**Lifeline**:
A once-per-Game aid: 50:50, Ask the Audience, Phone a Friend or Switch the Question.
_Avoid_: Power-up, hint, help

**Locked in**:
An answer confirmed with Final answer, held through the Reveal wait while nothing else can be played.
_Avoid_: Submitted, committed

**Reveal wait**:
The pause between Final answer and right or wrong showing, longer at each Tier. It can't be skipped.
_Avoid_: Delay, suspense pause

## Questions

**Question**:
A multiple-choice prompt with exactly four answers, one correct, belonging to one Topic file and one Tier.
_Avoid_: Card, item, trivia

**Tier**:
One of five difficulty grades, each matching three Rungs of the Ladder.
_Avoid_: Level, difficulty level

**Subject**:
The area of general knowledge a General Question belongs to, matching a Friend's specialty. Every TV and Movies Question counts as pop culture.
_Avoid_: Category (that's the top-level Topic choice), genre

**Friend**:
One of the four specialists the player can call with Phone a Friend: a history buff, a science buff, a sports fan or a pop-culture fan.
_Avoid_: Expert, helper

**Saved game**:
The one Game in progress kept on the device, so a refresh or Resume picks it up where it left off.
_Avoid_: Save file, save slot, session

**Seen question**:
A Question shown to the player on this device, whether answered or switched away from.
_Avoid_: Used question, answered question

## Sound

**Sting**:
A short sound for a moment in the Game: an answer Locked in, right or wrong, a Lifeline, a Walk away or winning the million.
_Avoid_: Sound effect, jingle

**Tier bed**:
The looping music under each Question, more suspenseful at each Tier, drawn out through the Reveal wait.
_Avoid_: Suspense bed, background music

**Menu theme**:
The looping music on the screens outside the Game.
_Avoid_: Title music, intro

**Sound switch**:
The control on the Main menu, Ready to play, the Game and Game over that turns all sound on or off. The device remembers it.
_Avoid_: Mute button, mute toggle

## Results

**Personal best**:
The highest winnings the player has reached in one Topic on this device.
_Avoid_: High score, record

**Share result**:
The copyable text summary of a finished Game.
_Avoid_: Scorecard, share card
